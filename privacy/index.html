Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Canopy Blocks (“the game”) is made by Marjanita (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to the game on iPhone and Android and to this website.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email or account, your game progress stays on your phone, and the game uses Google services to show ads, count how it is played and report crashes.

What the game collects

Game progress

Scores, lines cleared, garden stage and your settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Usage data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example when a game starts and ends, the score, the mode played and how long sessions last. Firebase also records your device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address) and a random app instance identifier. This data is not linked to your name, email or contacts.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with the device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the game at the moment of the crash and a random installation identifier.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The game downloads settings such as ad frequency and difficulty from Firebase Remote Config. To do this, Firebase uses the app instance identifier and basic device information. No game data is sent.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob, shown only between games. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and its partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app information, and which ads you see and tap. Depending on your choices (see below), this may be used to show personalised ads.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the game asks for your choice through Google’s consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy Choices in the game.
  • Tracking on iPhone. The game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Ads we do not allow

We block gambling, alcohol, dating, “get rich quick” and banking or financial-services ads, among other sensitive categories.

Why we use this data (legal bases)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, where consent is required.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, analytics, crash reports and remote settings: our legitimate interest in running, funding, fixing and improving a free game.

Who receives the data

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by Google LLC and its affiliates (Firebase and AdMob) as our service providers, and for ads also by Google’s advertising partners, under their own terms and policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, these transfers are protected by Google’s standard contractual clauses. Under some US state laws, showing personalised ads may count as “sharing” personal information; you can opt out with Settings > Privacy Choices or your phone’s ad settings.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Game progress: on your device until you uninstall the game.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time.

Because the game has no accounts, the data we hold is linked only to random identifiers on your device. To ask for deletion or a copy:

  1. Email marjanitallc@gmail.com with the subject “Canopy Blocks data request”.
  2. Tell us your phone type and roughly when you last played, so we can find the data.

We reply within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes all game data stored on your device. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub, which may log your IP address for security (GitHub Privacy Statement), and loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version here with a new date. For important changes we will also tell you in the game.
