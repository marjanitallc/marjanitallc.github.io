Real trees

Canopy Blocks grows a garden on your screen, and part of its ad income grows real trees.

Trees planted by Canopy Blocks players

Starting at launch

This counter is updated every month once the game is out, with a link to each planting.

How it works

  • Community trees. Part of the game’s ad income goes to planting trees for all players together.
  • Your own tree. When you have cleared 6,000 lines, your garden reaches the “Tree” stage and a real tree is planted for you. The goal is the same for players in every country.
  • A clear upper limit. Trees never take more than 10% of a month’s ad income. If more trees are earned than that, they are queued for the next month, never cancelled.
  • No purchase needed. Trees are funded only by ads. The game has no paid “plant a tree” button.

Our planting partner

We plan to plant through Tree-Nation, which runs reforestation projects around the world and publishes each planted tree with its project and location. We will link our Tree-Nation page here once planting starts.

Reports

Each month we will publish here the number of trees planted, the projects they went to and the share of ad income used.
