Canopy Blocks

A calm block puzzle where every line you clear helps a garden grow.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Coming soon to iPhone and Android.

Easy to learn

Drag blocks onto an 8×8 board and fill rows or columns to clear them. No timers, no pressure.

A garden that grows

Cleared lines bring your garden to life, from a bare hill to a full tree with flowers and butterflies.

Light on ads

Free to play. No ads during play and none in your first day; after that a short ad only between games, never more often than every third game.

Real trees

Part of the game's ad income goes to planting real trees. See how it works.
